# Asset credits

## Original project models

`models/weapons/`, `models/armor/`, `models/items/` and `models/furniture/` contain original artwork for this project,
generated by `tools/make_rifle.py`, `tools/make_gear.py`, `tools/make_items.py` and `tools/make_furniture.py`. They use no
third-party models or textures. The asset folders' READMEs record the palette, geometry budgets,
scene layout, and regeneration commands.

## Models (CC0 1.0, public domain)

By Quaternius (https://quaternius.com, https://www.patreon.com/quaternius). Free "Standard" versions.

| Folder | Pack |
|---|---|
| `models/characters/base/` | Universal Base Characters (base meshes and their textures, plus light skin variants) |
| `models/characters/hair/` | Universal Base Characters: hairstyles rigged to the head bone |
| `models/characters/outfits/`, `outfit_parts/` | Modular Character Outfits – Fantasy (whole outfits and separate parts, plus colour variants) |
| `models/characters/animations/` | Universal Animation Library (`UAL1_Standard.glb`; `_RM` has root motion baked in) |

`base/Superhero_Male_Head.gltf` and `Superhero_Female_Head.gltf` are cut from the base characters by
`tools/make_heads.py` (only the triangles bound to the head and neck bones), to wear under outfits.

Fix applied: the base character glTF files referred to `T_Eye_Normal_png.png` and
`T_Hair_1_Normal_png.png`, which the pack doesn't contain; they now use `T_Eye_Normal.png` and
`T_Hair_1_Normal.png`.

| `models/nature/` | Ultimate Nature Pack |
| `models/trees/` | Textured Stylized Trees (with its textures; `Tree_Bark.jpg` turned into PNG) |
| `models/crops/` | Nature Crops Pack |
| `models/village/buildings/`, `village/props/` | Medieval Village Pack |

These four packs only come as OBJ / FBX / .blend; `tools/obj_to_gltf.py` converts the OBJ files to
glTF (material colours from the MTL files, the trees' textures matched by material name).

The original downloads (FBX, OBJ, Blender files, previews, licences) are kept outside the game's
assets in `asset-sources/quaternius/`.
