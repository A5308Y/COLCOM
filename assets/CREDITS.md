# Asset credits

## Original project models

`models/weapons/`, `models/armor/`, `models/items/`, `models/furniture/`, `models/world/` and `models/plants/`
contain original artwork for this project, generated by `tools/make_rifle.py`, `tools/make_gear.py`,
`tools/make_items.py`, `tools/make_furniture.py`, `tools/make_world_props.py` and `tools/make_plants.py`. They use no
third-party models or textures, except the skinned apparel variants described below. The asset folders' READMEs record the palette, geometry budgets,
scene layout, and regeneration commands.

## Deforming clothing (CC0 derivatives)

`models/items/SimpleClothesMale/Female.gltf` and `ParkaMale/Female.gltf` are tailored derivatives
of Quaternius's Modular Character Outfits – Fantasy Peasant outfits and Universal Base Characters
(CC0, listed below).
`tools/make_skinned_clothes.py` builds new fabric fitted to the outfit's skeleton, retains its
bind poses and fits small sewn-on details to the new torso. Male variants retain the Peasant
outfit's bare forearms/hands and textures from `models/characters/outfits/`; female variants
include bare arms cut from `base/Superhero_Female_FullBody.gltf`, with its textures from
`models/characters/base/`. Fabric uses the project's own flat-color materials.

The continuous armor foundations in `models/armor/PoweredArmorMale.gltf` and
`PoweredArmorFemale.gltf` derive from the matching Universal Base Characters (CC0).
`tools/make_skinned_power_armor.py` retargets their skin weights to the outfit skeleton,
simplifies the anatomy and replaces all skin materials with opaque titanium,
including the neck covering and gloves. The armor plates and cyan materials are original project artwork.

The worn `CargoTrousersMale/Female.gltf` and `FlakLegGuardsMale/Female.gltf` in
`models/items/` reuse the matching CC0 Peasant outfit's trousers, boots and skin weights.
`tools/make_skinned_legs.py` adds original canvas pockets, raised ballistic guards and
flat-color project materials; it does not reuse the source textures.

## Models (CC0 1.0, public domain)

By Quaternius (https://quaternius.com, https://www.patreon.com/quaternius). Free "Standard" versions.

| Folder | Pack |
|---|---|
| `models/characters/base/` | Universal Base Characters (base meshes and their textures, plus light skin variants) |
| `models/characters/hair/` | Universal Base Characters: hairstyles rigged to the head bone |
| `models/characters/outfits/`, `outfit_parts/` | Modular Character Outfits – Fantasy (whole outfits and separate parts, plus colour variants) |
| `models/characters/animations/` | Universal Animation Library (`UAL1_Standard.glb`; `_RM` has root motion baked in) |

`base/Superhero_Male_Head.gltf` and `Superhero_Female_Head.gltf` are cut from the base characters by
`tools/make_heads.py` (only the triangles bound to the head and neck bones), to wear under outfits.

Fix applied: the base character glTF files referred to `T_Eye_Normal_png.png` and
`T_Hair_1_Normal_png.png`, which the pack doesn't contain; they now use `T_Eye_Normal.png` and
`T_Hair_1_Normal.png`.

| `models/nature/` | Ultimate Nature Pack |
| `models/trees/` | Textured Stylized Trees (with its textures; `Tree_Bark.jpg` turned into PNG) |
| `models/crops/` | Nature Crops Pack |
| `models/village/buildings/`, `village/props/` | Medieval Village Pack |

These four packs only come as OBJ / FBX / .blend; `tools/obj_to_gltf.py` converts the OBJ files to
glTF (material colours from the MTL files, the trees' textures matched by material name).

The original downloads (FBX, OBJ, Blender files, previews, licences) are kept outside the game's
assets in `asset-sources/quaternius/`.
